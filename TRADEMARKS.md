## 3. Uses That Do Not Require Permission

Subject to this policy and applicable law, prior written permission is generally not required for the following uses.

### 3.1 Referring to the Official Project

You may use the Marks to accurately refer to the official Monogatari Assistant project.

Examples include:

* "Monogatari Assistant is licensed under the Apache License 2.0."
* "This plugin works with Monogatari Assistant."
* "This document explains how to configure MonoAshi."
* "This project was originally derived from Monogatari Assistant."

The Marks should be used only as necessary to identify the project and should not be presented more prominently than the name or branding of your own product or project.

### 3.2 Compatibility Statements

You may make truthful statements describing compatibility with Monogatari Assistant.

Examples include:

* "Compatible with Monogatari Assistant"
* "Plugin for Monogatari Assistant"
* "Supports Monogatari Assistant projects"
* "Designed to work with MonoAshi"

Such statements must not imply that the product is developed, certified, sponsored, approved, or endorsed by Heyairu（部屋伊琉） unless written permission has been granted.

### 3.3 Describing Forks and Derivative Works

You may truthfully state that a project is based on, derived from, or forked from Monogatari Assistant.

Examples include:

* "Based on Monogatari Assistant"
* "Forked from Monogatari Assistant"
* "Derived from the Monogatari Assistant project"

However, the name of the modified project itself must be sufficiently distinguishable from the Monogatari Assistant Marks.

### 3.4 Commentary, Journalism, Education, and Community Discussion

You may use the Marks when discussing Monogatari Assistant in:

* articles;
* reviews;
* tutorials;
* academic works;
* presentations;
* videos;
* community discussions;

Such use must be truthful and must not misleadingly imply official affiliation or endorsement.
